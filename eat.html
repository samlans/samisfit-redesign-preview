Tools, reconciled: design-review backs E1, calories + macros in one read then meals is the fastest, most legible log (Nielsen #6, and clear unit labels). impeccable likes the E2 ring as the brand-energy moment but warns a ring hides the per-gram numbers a tracker needs, so keep it as a header, not the whole screen. E3's coach line and quick-add chips are the differentiator (this is a coached app), worth folding in. Verdict: E1 as the spine, with E3's coach line + quick-add chips, and E2's ring available as a compact header variant. Monogram food chips replace emoji everywhere.
