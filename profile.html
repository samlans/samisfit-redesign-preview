Tools, reconciled: design-review rates Pr2 highest for an analytics home, labelled sections and clear units beat a dense tile wall for comprehension (Nielsen #8, aesthetic & minimalist, and #6 recognition), and it wants the est-1RM gains near the top since that is the headline a lifter checks. impeccable pushes back that a pure feed under-uses the brand, and loves Pr1's bento energy but flags the risk of the hero-metric cliche in Pr3 if the big number is vanity. Verdict: Pr2 as the spine (strength gains first), with Pr1's colour tiles for the top-line stats, a feed with bento at the top. Pr3's single hero only if the number is always meaningful (streak works; a vanity total does not). Your call on which to converge.
